Privacy Policy for Merge Mob

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Merge Mob (the “App”), provided by Abu Butt (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to play Merge Mob. We do not directly request this information through the App.

The App uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. When you use the App, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including an advertising ID where available;
  • IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device type, operating system, and language;
  • Advertising impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information.

These activities are controlled in part by Google and its partners and are subject to their privacy practices and your applicable consent choices.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through our advertising service may be used to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and, when permitted by applicable law and your choices, personalize advertising.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob, a Google advertising service. Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers, IP addresses, cookies or similar technologies, where applicable, to provide and measure advertisements. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps or sites that use its services

We do not sell personal information directly. Advertising partners may process information for advertising purposes as described above. Certain disclosures or uses for targeted advertising may qualify as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws.

4. Your Privacy Choices

Where required, we provide a consent or privacy choices message for advertising. You can accept, decline, or manage choices as made available in that message. Where applicable, you may also adjust advertising preferences in your device settings, including resetting or limiting your advertising ID.

To ask about the information handled in connection with Merge Mob or exercise applicable privacy rights, contact us using the details below. For information controlled by Google, please also consult Google's privacy controls.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or a separate database of personal profiles for Merge Mob. Information processed by AdMob is retained according to Google's and relevant advertising partners' retention policies. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of any personal information we control, where applicable. We will review and respond to requests under applicable law.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to configure our services responsibly. Third-party services, including Google AdMob, use their own safeguards. No method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Merge Mob is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other contact details from children through the App. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the request. If the App is directed to children or includes children in its intended audience, we will update this policy and apply the relevant age-appropriate advertising settings.

8. International Data Processing

Google and its advertising partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their applicable privacy policies and legal obligations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date.
